Our name

What Rahma Means

Rahma is an Arabic word for mercy — not the grand, abstract kind, but the everyday kind: a seat saved for you, a question taken seriously, a door held open. It’s the community we hope to build, one gathering at a time.

What would our neighborhoods look like if we made more room for one another? We’re here to find out.

Featured program

Shaah & Caano

Books, Tea, Reflection, and Sisterhood.

Twice a month, women gather around a shared table — shaah in hand, a book open, conversation unhurried. It’s more than a book club. It’s a place to read together, ask real questions, laugh, reflect on faith and life, and build friendships that outlast the meeting.

Currently reading: Women Around the Messenger by Muhammad ‘Ali Qutb — Saturday, August 15 at La Jolla Beach: book club, beach day, and a seashell mirror craft.

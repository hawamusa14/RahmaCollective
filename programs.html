Featured program

Shaah & Caano

Books, Tea, Reflection, and Sisterhood.

Twice a month, women gather around a shared table to read, reflect, and talk about the things that actually matter — over shaah and caano, of course. It’s a book club the way a home is a building: technically true, and not the point.

What happens here: bi-weekly book selections and discussions, themed gatherings, guest conversations, community outings, reflection prompts — and the kind of friendships that start over tea.

What we read: fiction, nonfiction, memoir, poetry, personal development, Islamic literature, cultural works, social commentary. If it sparks a real conversation, it belongs on the table.

Currently reading: Women Around the Messenger by Muhammad ‘Ali Qutb — Saturday, August 15 at La Jolla Beach: book club, beach day, and a seashell mirror craft. We just recently finished reading The Barakah Effect by Mohammed A. Faris.
